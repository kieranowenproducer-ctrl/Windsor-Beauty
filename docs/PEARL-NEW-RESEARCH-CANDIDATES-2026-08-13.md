## Why these are worth reviewing

PEARL's approved tertiary record links NAC with OCD, but one PubMed link supplied beside that claim was unrelated. These three genuine papers show why the answer needs to remain cautious: results are mixed rather than uniformly positive.

1. **Large negative trial, 2022**
   - 98 adults, 20 weeks, 2 to 4 g/day NAC as an add-on.
   - Found no evidence of improved OCD symptoms over placebo.
   - https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35304155/

2. **Systematic review and meta-analysis, 2024**
   - Six randomized trials, 195 adults.
   - Reported a possible benefit at five to eight weeks, but not at shorter or longer periods, and called for more trials.
   - https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39376972/

3. **Small controlled trial, 2026**
   - 35 adults, 12 weeks, 2,400 mg/day NAC added to sertraline.
   - Reported faster early improvement, but the sample was small and the placebo response was also high.
   - https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42260157/
